Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Samarth Auto Reply is a personal automation project intended to help the account operator manage and respond to messages received by their connected Instagram professional account. This policy explains the information the app may process and how it is used.

Information processed

When enabled and authorized, the automation may process Instagram account identifiers, sender identifiers, message text sent to the connected account, message timestamps, and technical execution data needed to process an event and generate a response. The app is intended to use only information made available through authorized platform integrations.

How information is used

Information is used to receive messaging events, generate a response, deliver a reply when that feature is enabled, troubleshoot errors, and protect the workflow from misuse. The app operator should not use the automation to collect information unrelated to these purposes.

AI processing and service providers

If an AI provider is connected to the workflow, message text and necessary context may be sent to that provider to generate a reply. Information may also be processed by Meta and by the workflow hosting provider. Those services process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Disclosure and sale

The app operator does not intend to sell personal information. Information may be disclosed to the service providers described above when necessary to operate the automation, comply with law, address security issues, or protect rights and safety.

Retention and security

Execution logs and message data should be retained only for as long as needed to operate, secure, or troubleshoot the automation. Access tokens and API credentials should be stored in secure credential storage and must not be published in code, logs, or public repositories. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and privacy requests

If you have a privacy question or request concerning a message handled by this automation, contact the app operator using the contact email listed in the app's Meta settings. You may also use Instagram's own controls to manage your interactions with the connected account.

Children's privacy

This project is not designed to collect information from children under 13, and the operator does not knowingly seek to collect such information.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be available at this page.
